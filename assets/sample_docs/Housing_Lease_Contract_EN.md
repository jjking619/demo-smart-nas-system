# Housing Lease Contract (Sample)

> This file is a fictional sample contract for NAS-Demo testing purposes only. It is used to validate knowledge base retrieval and has no legal effect.

## Article 1 Contract Information

- Contract No.: XZ-2026-0901
- Signing Date: March 1, 2026
- Lessor (Party A): Wang Jianguo
- Lessee (Party B): Li Xiaofang

## Article 2 Property Information

Party A agrees to lease the property owned by Party A to Party B:

- Property Address: Kejiyuan Road, Nanshan District, Shenzhen
- Purpose of Use: Residential
- Floor Area: 89.5 square meters

## Article 3 Lease Term

1. The lease term is 12 months, from April 1, 2026 to March 31, 2027.
2. Upon expiration, Party A may repossess the property and Party B shall return it on time. If Party B requests renewal, written notice must be provided to Party A at least one month before expiration. Under the same conditions, Party B has priority to renew the lease.

## Article 4 Rent and Payment Terms

1. Monthly rent is CNY 6,500.
2. Payment terms: two-month deposit and one-month rent in advance. Party B shall transfer rent to the account designated by Party A within five days before the start of each rental period.
3. During the lease term, utility and service charges including water, electricity, gas, property management, and internet fees shall be borne by Party B and settled monthly according to actual usage.

## Article 5 Security Deposit

1. On the signing date of this contract, Party B shall pay Party A a security deposit of CNY 13,000.
2. At lease expiration, after Party B settles all fees and returns the property as agreed, Party A shall refund the deposit without interest within seven days.
3. If Party B has unpaid fees or unrepaired damage, Party A may deduct the corresponding amount from the deposit.

## Article 6 Property Maintenance and Repairs

1. Party A shall ensure the property structure and facilities meet safety requirements related to construction, fire prevention, public security, and hygiene, and shall not endanger personal safety.
2. During the lease term, repairs caused by natural wear and tear or within Party A's responsibility shall be handled and paid by Party A.
3. Repairs caused by improper use or intentional/negligent damage by Party B shall be handled or compensated by Party B.

## Article 7 Termination and Breach Liability

1. Party A may unilaterally terminate this contract, repossess the property, and retain the deposit if Party B:
   - Changes the property use without authorization, or subleases/lends the property;
   - Uses the property for illegal activities;
   - Delays rent payment for more than 15 cumulative days.
2. If Party A repossesses the property before lease expiration, Party A shall provide one-month written notice, refund unused rent and full deposit, and pay Party B liquidated damages equal to one month's rent.
3. If Party B terminates the lease early, Party B shall provide one-month written notice. Paid rent is non-refundable, and the deposit will be handled according to this contract.

## Article 8 Miscellaneous

1. Party B shall take proper care of the property and its facilities, and shall not alter the structure without written consent from Party A.
2. If the property is affected by force majeure events such as government requisition or demolition during the lease term, neither party bears breach liability, and rent shall be settled by actual days of use.
3. This contract is made in duplicate, with one copy held by each party, and takes effect on the date both parties sign.
